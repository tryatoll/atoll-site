How it works

One hold. The rest takes care of itself.

No schedules to plan and no timers to start. Lock when you want to focus, and Atoll fills in the rest of your day.

1

Pick what to block

Apps, whole categories and websites. Games and Entertainment are in from the start, and the ones you use most sit at the top.

2

Hold to lock

Choose 30, 60, 90 or 120 minutes, or no limit. When the time is up, your apps open again on their own.

3

See your day

Every lock grows forest on your ring. Phone time, sleep and workouts fill in without you lifting a finger.

Your day at a glance

Your whole day on one ring.

Land is the day so far, forest is the time you focused, and gaps are long stretches on your phone. Sleep and workouts circle the outside.

  • Sleep and workouts, already there. They come straight from Apple Health.
  • Nothing to type. Your phone already knows how the day went.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

When is Atoll coming out?

We are aiming for December 2026 on iPhone. Leave your email above and we will tell you the day it reaches the App Store.

How is this different from Screen Time?

Screen Time sets limits. Atoll shows you the whole day: when you focused, when you drifted, how you slept and moved, all on one ring. Locking takes one hold.

Do I need to set anything up?

Pick the apps to block once. After that, just hold to lock when you want to focus.

What if I need my phone?

Hold for five seconds and the session ends, any time. With a time goal, your apps also open on their own when it is up.

Where does my sleep data come from?

From Apple Health, if you allow it. Without it, Atoll estimates sleep from when you put your phone down.

Which apps and sites can it block?

Whatever you choose. Atoll uses Apple's Screen Time framework to block the apps and categories you pick, plus the websites you list.

Is it free?

Yes. Free covers locking, today's ring and the last 7 days. Pro keeps your full history. See Free & Pro above.

What do I need to run it?

An iPhone on iOS 26.4 or later.

Does it work on Android?

Not yet. iPhone comes first.

What do you do with my data?

Your app usage stays on your iPhone. We use your email only to tell you about Atoll. The details are in the privacy policy.

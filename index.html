FAQ

Before you ask.

When is Atoll coming out?

We're aiming for November 2026 on iPhone. Leave your email and we'll tell you the day.

How is this different from Screen Time?

Screen Time counts the scrolling. Atoll shows the whole day on one island, and locking takes one hold.

Will it make me feel bad about my day?

No scores, no guilt trips. Atoll shows the day as it was.

Do I need to set anything up?

Pick the apps to block once. After that, just hold to lock.

What if I need my phone?

Hold to end whenever you like. With a goal, your apps also open when time is up.

Where does my sleep data come from?

Apple Health, if you allow it. Without it, Atoll estimates from when you put your phone down.

Which apps and sites can it block?

Whatever you choose, through Apple's Screen Time framework, plus the sites you list.

Is it free?

Yes. Free covers locking, today, the last 7 days and sharing. Pro adds full history and stat stickers.

What do I need to run it?

An iPhone on iOS 26.4 or later.

What do you do with my data?

Usage stays on your iPhone. We use your email only to tell you about Atoll. See the privacy policy.

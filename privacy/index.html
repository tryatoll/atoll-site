Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026

Atoll ("we", "us") is an iPhone app that shows your day as a single ring and can lock distracting apps and websites. We are based in Sydney, Australia. This page explains what we collect, why, who helps us handle it, and how you can have it removed. You can reach us any time at hello@tryatoll.app.

The app is not released yet. Right now we only collect the email addresses people leave on this website. The sections about the app describe what it will do once it is available, and we will update this page if that changes.

1. This website

If you sign up for launch news, we store your email address and the time you signed up. We use it to tell you about Atoll, such as the launch or a beta invitation. We do not sell it, share it for advertising or use it for anything else. To be removed, email us and we will delete it.

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to see aggregate numbers such as how many people visit and which pages they open. It does not use cookies and does not track you across other sites. Our hosting and infrastructure providers may also log technical data such as your IP address when you visit. We do not use any of this to identify you.

2. The app: what we collect

Account. You sign in with Apple. We receive a user ID, and the name and email address Apple shares with us. If you choose "Hide My Email", we only see Apple's private relay address. We also keep the token Apple gives us at sign-in, only so we can cancel it when you delete your account.

What you set up. Your modes (name, icon, how you unlock), the websites you choose to block, the apps you choose to block (kept in a form from Apple that we cannot read), and the NFC cards you register (the card's ID number, the name and icon you give it, how often you used it).

Lock history. For each lock: when it started and ended, your goal end time, how it ended, what started it, the mode's name at the time, and how many apps and sites it blocked.

Daily summary. Rough time ranges of when you likely slept and when you used your phone for a long stretch, worked out from Screen Time. We do not store which apps you used in those ranges. This lets your stats survive a reinstall.

Settings. Your preferences, such as Strict mode and notification options, and how many emergency unlocks you used this month.

Purchases. If you buy Atoll Pro, Apple handles the payment. We use RevenueCat to know whether your purchase is active. It receives your account ID and Apple's purchase information. We never see your card details.

Crash and error reports. We use Sentry to find bugs. A report can include the app version, iOS version, device model and what the app was doing when it failed. We configure it not to send your name, email, account ID, the apps or sites you block, or screenshots.

Usage statistics. We use TelemetryDeck to count how the app is used, for example whether people finish setup or open the stats screen. The signals carry an anonymous ID that cannot be traced back to you. They never include your name, email, blocked apps or sites, or anything from Health.

3. What stays on your phone

How long you used each app. Atoll reads Screen Time with your permission to draw your stats, and the per-app numbers are never uploaded.

Sleep and workouts from Apple Health. If you allow it, Atoll reads them to draw your ring. They are shown on your phone only. We do not upload them, share them or use them for ads, and nothing is written to Health.

4. How we use it

Only to run the app: to sign you in, keep your modes and history, draw your stats, restore your data on a new phone, confirm your purchase and fix bugs. We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or track you across other companies' apps and websites.

5. Who handles it for us

Apple provides Sign in with Apple, the App Store, Screen Time and Health. Their own privacy policy applies to what they handle.

Supabase stores your account and app data. The data is held in South Korea (Seoul), and each person can reach only their own records.

RevenueCat (United States) manages purchase status. Sentry (hosted in the European Union) receives crash and error reports. TelemetryDeck (Germany) receives anonymous usage statistics.

For this website, Cloudflare runs our DNS, the service that records launch sign-ups, the privacy-friendly visit statistics, and forwards mail sent to our address. GitHub hosts this website. Resend sends email on our behalf, such as launch news.

Because of these providers, your information may be processed outside the country where you live, including in South Korea, the European Union and the United States. We do not share it with anyone else, unless the law requires it.

6. How long we keep it, and deleting it

Launch sign-ups are kept until you ask us to remove them or we have sent the launch news and you no longer want to hear from us. App data is kept until you delete your account. You can do that inside the app from your account in Settings. It removes your data from our server, deletes your record at RevenueCat, cancels the Sign in with Apple token and clears the app's data on your phone. You can also email us and we will delete it for you.

Purchase records may stay with Apple as long as accounting and legal rules require. If you reinstall and use "Restore Purchases", Apple can confirm what you bought. Crash reports and usage statistics are kept for a limited time by Sentry and TelemetryDeck and then removed.

7. Your choices and rights

You can see and change your modes and cards in the app. Depending on where you live, you may also ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, to delete it, or to object to how we use it. Email us and we will reply within 30 days. We handle personal information in line with applicable privacy law, including Australia's Privacy Act 1988 where it applies. If you think we got it wrong, you can contact your local privacy regulator, such as the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

8. Permissions the app asks for

Screen Time access lets Atoll block apps and websites and read how long you use your phone. Without it, blocking and stats do not work. Health access (read only: sleep and workouts) lets Atoll draw them around your ring, and it is optional. Notifications tell you when a lock or break ends, and they are optional. NFC lets Atoll read the ID number of the cards you choose to register. It never writes anything to your cards. You can change any of these in iOS Settings.

9. Children

Atoll is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

10. Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here and update the date at the top. If the change is significant, we will tell you in the app or by email.
